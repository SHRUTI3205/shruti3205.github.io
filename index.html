01 / About

A bit about me.

I'm Shruti, a Data Science student at S.B. Jain Institute in Nagpur, graduating in 2027. What I enjoy most is the middle of a project, when a pile of raw data slowly starts to make sense and you can finally see what it's been saying all along.

Most of my projects started with a small question. Can everyday behaviour hint that someone is getting stressed? What kinds of customers does a mall actually have? That's how I ended up building a stress early-warning tool, a customer segmentation dashboard and a store rating app. None of them is perfect, and I've tried to be upfront about what each one can and can't do, but I learned something real from every one.

Apart from coursework, I did the Google Android developer virtual internship in early 2025, and this year I've been picking up certifications in analytics, machine learning and cybersecurity. Right now I'm looking for an internship where I can work with people who know more than I do, and be useful while I learn.
